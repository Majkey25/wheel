Privacy and terms

Updated 5 October 2026.

Operator and contact

Spin the Wheel is maintained by Matěj Teplý, publishing as Majkey. Contact: majkeylab@gmail.com. Source code.

Privacy and browser storage

Choices and random selections are processed in your browser and are not sent to an application server. Your choices are saved under wheelOptions in localStorage so they remain available next time you open the wheel. They remain until you use Clear, remove all choices or clear the site's browser data. Clear removes the saved choices after confirmation. Avoid names or sensitive information on shared devices.

The app has no accounts, analytics, advertisements or tracking cookies and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. Browser storage is used only to retain your chosen wheel inputs. Other pages on the same majkey25.github.io origin can access this storage, so do not treat it as private or encrypted storage.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors' IP addresses for security. For its retention, international transfers and privacy rights, see the GitHub privacy statement and Pages data collection.

Contact messages and deletion

If you email the operator, your address and message are used to answer your request, on the basis of the legitimate interest in providing support. Messages are kept only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Email is handled through Gmail under Google's privacy policy. Do not send unnecessary personal information, including children's information.

Use Clear in the wheel to delete your saved choices. The operator cannot access or erase your browser storage remotely. Request access, correction or deletion of information held by the operator, or object to its processing, using the email above. Other applicable rights include restriction and portability. You can complain to your data protection authority, including the Czech ÚOOÚ. For GitHub records, use the contacts in GitHub's privacy statement.

Terms of use and fees

The wheel is free. The operator offers no purchases or subscriptions and charges no fees, so there is no paid order to cancel or refund. It is a casual decision aid using browser pseudorandom numbers. It is not a certified lottery, gambling service or auditable prize-draw system. Use it lawfully and avoid relying on it for safety-critical decisions.
